Manual exception
  crops_leafsStageA.glb and crops_leafsStageB.glb: the manifest keep regex ("leaf") matches the node name, so run_all passed the whole mesh through untouched and the validator failed (UVs outside 0..1, 0 voxels). Re-running run_all on these files would fail again and delete the output; fix the keep regex in build_manifest.py (WP0) first.

Deviations to decide
  1. Scaled variants (k != 1) are a new mechanism: the doc remap rule picks ids by hash without scaling, which cannot hit +-25 % for every source. Variants keep the call-site scales and the triangle counts.
  2. massif_1..4 are generated at cell 2.5-3.0 m (30-57 m tall, 1.7k-3.0k tris); the spec cell 0.9-1.2 was not measured (estimated well above the 3k budget). Not mapped to any source.
  3. tree_swamp_l1 is generated but no source is classified into the swamp group (the group exists in build_replace.py for the marsh region).
  4. crops_dirt* keep the 0.15 game cell from the spec; they are 0.2 m tall in game, so they become one or two voxel layers.
